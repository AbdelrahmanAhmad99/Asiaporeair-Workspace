Where to find your reference

  • Booking Confirmation Email Look for "Booking Reference" or "PNR" near the top of your confirmation email from Asiaporeair.
  • Printed or Digital Itinerary The 6-character alphanumeric code appears in the header section of your travel itinerary document.
  • Travel Agency Confirmation If booked through a travel agent, your booking reference will appear on the agency's booking receipt.
