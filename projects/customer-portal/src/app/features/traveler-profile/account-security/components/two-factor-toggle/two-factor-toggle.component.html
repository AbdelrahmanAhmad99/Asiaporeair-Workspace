Two-Factor Authentication requires a one-time verification code from your authenticator app each time you access your suite. This prevents unauthorised logins even if your password is compromised.

  • Protects against credential theft and phishing attacks
  • Compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, and 1Password
  • Instant alerts on unrecognised login attempts
